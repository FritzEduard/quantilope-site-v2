The product

Aemulus-XR, a company founded by Sam Gebhardt and Paul Bernal, builds augmented reality training for pre-hospital care. An AI acts as the instructor, drawing on Clinical Practice Guidelines (CPGs). The platform is built on MetaHuman and Unreal Engine, for the Meta Quest 3 headset.

Target markets

According to Aemulus-XR: military medical training (TCCC: Tactical Combat Casualty Care, tiers 1 to 4), law-enforcement medical training (TECC-LEO), civilian emergency medical services (EMS), Objective Structured Clinical Examinations (OSCE) and telehealth.

Our role

Aemulus-XR remains the owner of the product. The founder of Solutions Quantilope is listed on the Aemulus-XR Team page as a software engineer with a strong background in video game development and immersive technologies, alongside co-founders Sam Gebhardt and Paul Bernal, James King and Walter Engle (Chief Medical Officer).
