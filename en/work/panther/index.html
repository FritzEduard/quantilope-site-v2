Panther Maintenance Simulator

VR maintenance simulator for the Dauphin-Panther helicopter

Client
French Navy, Technical Training Unit (S.I.T.)
Sector
Industry and defense
Platform
PCVR · Pico 4 Enterprise

Context

Training technicians on Dauphin-Panther maintenance means grounding a real aircraft, which carries a high operating cost for the training unit.

The project

A VR simulator that reproduces the helicopter faithfully through photogrammetry. Trainees face several failure scenarios that they must diagnose, then repair by following the correct maintenance procedure.

Technologies

  • PCVR
  • Pico 4 Enterprise
  • Unity
  • C#
  • OpenXR
  • XR Interaction Toolkit
  • 3ds Max
  • Photogrammetry
