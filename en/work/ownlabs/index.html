Work / Ownlabs

Delivered

Ownlabs

A virtual science lab on a smartphone and a low-cost VR headset

Owner
Ownlabs team (École Supérieure Polytechnique de Dakar and University of Ziguinchor)
Sector
Education
Location
Dakar, Senegal (founded in 2017)

Context

Ownlabs is a virtual science lab (physics, chemistry, biology) created in 2017 by Abdou Khadre Diop and a team of students. It aims to fill the gap left by the lack of physical labs in Senegalese schools, using just a smartphone and a low-cost VR headset.

Our contribution

The founder of Solutions Quantilope took part in the product’s technical development.

Recognition

Winner of the 2018 Ericsson Innovation Awards (grand prize for the Middle East & Africa region, then internationally) and finalist for the 2017 Orange Social Entrepreneur Prize in Africa and the Middle East.
