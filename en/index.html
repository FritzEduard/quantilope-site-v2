Why immersion

Training for mistakes is costly, dangerous, or impossible. Not in simulation.

  • Without exposing anyone

    Practice critical procedures, including those where a real mistake would be serious, without endangering people or equipment.

  • Without grounding equipment

    Train without taking an aircraft or machine out of service. The Panther simulator illustrates it: the helicopter stays available while the trainee practices on it in virtual reality.

  • As many times as needed

    Replay a scenario under identical conditions until the procedure is mastered.

Method

A four-step method

From the training need to deployment on headset.

  1. 01

    Scoping

    Learning objectives, procedures to teach, equipment and site constraints.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    A key scenario playable on headset, to validate the approach before producing.

  3. 03

    Production

    3D content, interactions, scenarios and visual feedback, iterated with your teams.

  4. 04

    Deployment

    Installation on headsets, trainer onboarding, updates.
